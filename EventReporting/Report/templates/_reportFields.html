Event Type Definitions
Near Miss
An event that relates to a specific patient but did not reach or affect the patient.
Example: Absence of kidney blocking on a spine field, caught by pretreatment imaging and corrected before delivery.
Unsafe Condition
A condition that increases the probability of a safety event; not related to a specific patient.
Example: No policy on allowed IGRT alignment tolerances for SBRT, causing confusion among staff.
Process Improvement
Not directly related to safety but contributes to inefficiency or confusion.
Example: Physician not alerted that the patient is ready, delaying that patient and those that follow.
Therapeutic Radiation Incident
Delivery of some part of a therapeutic dose not as intended, with or without harm.
Examples: Improper targeting delivering dose to a benign nodule; overdose from a commissioning error; one fraction delivered at the wrong SSD causing a 2 Gy discrepancy.
Other Safety Incident
Directly affects a patient but does not involve therapeutic radiation dose, with or without harm.
Examples: Patient fall; gantry collision with patient.
